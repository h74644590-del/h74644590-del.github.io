Privacy Policy

KlippQuest only accesses the YouTube channels owned by Chaos Clips, to upload videos and read view counts. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising. Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's computer. To revoke access, visit myaccount.google.com/permissions.
